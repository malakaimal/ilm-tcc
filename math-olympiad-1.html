Foundational mathematics challenge

About the Programme

ILM Math Olympiad 1 Batch-1 2027 is designed to encourage students to understand mathematical ideas, strengthen number sense and apply classroom learning to interesting problems. The focus is on clear thinking and learning, not memorising methods alone.

Students are encouraged to read questions carefully, explore different approaches and explain how they reach an answer.

🔢

Core Concepts

Practise number operations, patterns, basic geometry and other age-appropriate mathematical ideas.

🧠

Logical Thinking

Develop reasoning skills by recognising relationships, comparing information and solving problems step by step.

🌱

Learning Confidence

Build confidence through practice, constructive feedback and a positive competition experience.

02 • SUBJECTS & PAPER PATTERN

What will students be tested on?

The announced paper pattern contains 80 multiple-choice questions across two subject groups.

🧮

Mathematics & IQ

50 MCQs

Questions are intended to assess mathematical understanding, numerical skills, patterns and logical reasoning.

📖

English Grammar

30 MCQs

Questions focus on English grammar knowledge and language accuracy appropriate to the participating classes.
